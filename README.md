## 📂 Overview
z-mail-agent is an extensible AI email framework designed to automate your email management. It classifies and auto-replies to unread messages using email labels for efficient processing. This tool will simplify your email experience and help you stay organized.

## 🔍 Features
- **Automatic Classification**: Sorts your emails using AI to help you find important messages quickly.
- **Smart Auto-replies**: Responds to emails automatically based on your predefined settings.
- **Email Labeling**: Uses labels to track and manage the state of your emails.
- **User-friendly Interface**: No programming knowledge required; designed for everyone.
